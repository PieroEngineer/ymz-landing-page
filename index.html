Your data will be stored in a digital database named "Yummizi LP" owned by Yummizi. We will retain this information for a period of 2 years or until you formally revoke your consent, whichever occurs first.

To ensure the functionality of this website, we utilize third-party infrastructure (netlify and supabase). By submitting your data, you acknowledge and consent to this international transfer under adequate security standards.

In accordance with international privacy standards and Peruvian Law No. 29733, you have the right to Access, Rectify, Cancel, or Oppose (ARCO) the processing of your data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us at: yummizi.mkt@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request."

Mandatory Nature of Information

Providing the requested data is mandatory to join our waiting list. Failure to provide this information will prevent us from contacting you or providing updates regarding the launch.
